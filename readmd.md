### AngelWing Converter
This is a simple utility to convert Final Fantasy VIII's Project Angelwing mod to the game's remastered version. Requires FF8_demaster (see <https://github.com/MaKiPL/FF8_demaster>).

This was quickly thrown together for my personal use, but if you want to use it on your end, adjust the filepath in main.py to match your game's directory. Once started, the program will likely take several minutes to finish converting all of the relevant files.
